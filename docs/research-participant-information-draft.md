# Draft Humor Study participant information for Columbia review

This draft helps the Columbia research team prepare participant information that matches the website. It is not an approved consent form and must not be used to enroll participants. The operator confirmed that research consent, the investigator contact, and retention/sharing rules have not been prepared. Bracketed items need the investigator’s decisions and any applicable IRB review before publication.

Columbia must determine the applicable consent, waiver, or exemption requirements. Even where research is exempt, Columbia recommends appropriate participant information for direct interaction. The website’s age and Terms checkbox does not establish research consent. See [Columbia’s current informed consent policy](https://research.columbia.edu/sites/research.columbia.edu/files/content/HRPO/Policies%20%26%20Guidance/Informed%20Consent%20Policy%2020260201%20final%20clean.pdf) and its [protocol and consent resources](https://research.columbia.edu/irb-protocol-and-consent-form-resources).
